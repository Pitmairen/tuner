Privacy Policy

Tuner (Android app net.myr1.tuner) · Effective 15 October 2026

In short: Tuner listens through your microphone to detect notes and chords. All of that happens on your phone. The app doesn't collect, store or send any personal data, has no accounts, ads or analytics, and doesn't connect to the internet. Audio only leaves your phone if you choose to share a clip.

Microphone

Tuner needs microphone access to hear your instrument. The audio is analysed in real time on your device to find the pitch of the note or the chord you play. It is not recorded to storage and is never sent anywhere by the app.

While the app is open, it keeps the most recent 10 seconds of microphone audio in the phone's memory, so you can share a clip if detection goes wrong (see below). This audio is continuously overwritten, is not saved to storage, and is discarded when the app closes.

Sharing an audio clip

In Settings, “Share last 10 seconds” saves those 10 seconds as an audio file in the app's temporary cache and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose whether to send it and where to (for example email or a messaging app). The developer only receives a clip if you send it to them, and uses it solely to improve the app's note and chord detection. Anywhere you send it, the recipient's own privacy policy applies. Your phone may delete the cached file at any time.

Data stored on your device

Tuner saves your settings on your phone: the selected tuning, reference pitch, capo, display options and any custom tunings you create. It also caches the reference tones it plays. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What Tuner does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party services that collect data.
  • No internet connection: the app does not send data to any server.

Children

Tuner doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
