# Google Play listing and Play Console answers

Draft text and form answers for the Play Console. Character limits are
Google's; counts are noted where they matter.

## Store listing

**App name** (max 30): `Tuner – Guitar, Bass & Ukulele` (30)

The installed app's label stays "Tuner" (`app.json` → `name`); the store title
can be longer and helps people find it.

**Short description** (max 80):

> Tune guitar, bass & ukulele, play reference notes and name the chords you play

(78)

**Full description** (max 4000):

> Tuner is a clean, accurate tuner for guitar, bass and ukulele that also
> recognises the chords you play.
>
> TUNE
> • A clear needle shows how far you are from the note, in cents
> • Detects which string you're playing, or lock onto one string
> • ✓ marks each string as it gets in tune, with a little celebration when they all are
> • Hints when you're playing the right note in the wrong octave
> • Chromatic mode for any note on any instrument
>
> TUNINGS
> • Guitar: Standard, Drop D, Half/Full Step Down, Drop C, DADGAD, Open G, D, E and C
> • Bass: 4-string, Drop D, Half Step Down and 5-string
> • Ukulele: Standard (re-entrant), Low G, Baritone and D tuning
> • Create and save your own custom tunings
> • Capo / transpose and reference pitch (A4 = 415–450 Hz)
>
> REFERENCE NOTES
> • Play each string's note, or strum the whole tuning, to tune by ear
>
> CHORDS
> • Strum and see the chord name: major, minor, 7ths, sus, add9, dim, aug and power chords
> • Follows along as you play a song, with a history of the chords you played
> • Optional chord diagrams for guitar and ukulele, matched to the shape you're playing
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> • Everything happens on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet connection
>
> Tip: in a noisy room, hold the phone close to the sound hole or the amp.

(≈1,270)

## App content (Policy → App content)

**Privacy policy:** URL above.

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**App access:** All functionality is available without special access (no
login).

**Content rating** (IARC questionnaire): category "Utility, Productivity,
Communication, or Other"; answer No to violence, sexuality, language,
controlled substances, gambling, user interaction/sharing of content between
users, location sharing and purchases. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

**Target audience:** 13 and over is the simplest choice. Including under-13s
makes the app subject to the Designed for Families policy and extra review;
possible later since the app collects no data and has no ads.

**Data safety:**
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
  - Microphone audio is processed only on the device and is not collected
    (Google: data processed ephemerally on-device and never sent off it doesn't
    count as collected).
  - "Share last 10 seconds" is a user-initiated share through the Android
    share sheet to an app and recipient the user picks; the app itself
    doesn't transmit it.
- Is all data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data transmitted).
- Account deletion: not applicable (no accounts).

**News app / COVID-19 / Government / Financial features / Health:** No.

**Permissions** declared by the app: microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`), audio
settings, vibration, internet (normal permission; unused by the release app).
No restricted permissions, no foreground services.
